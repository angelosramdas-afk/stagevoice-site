Questions, answered.

What is StageVoice?

StageVoice is a teleprompter app for iPhone and Android that follows your voice. As you read, it listens through your phone's microphone, matches the words you say to your script, and scrolls so your current line stays in the reading guide. It's for anyone who speaks in front of people, from a class presentation to a keynote.

Can students use StageVoice for class presentations?

Yes. Students use it to practise and to present: paste your presentation script, then read it with Voice Tracking or Auto-Scroll so you never lose your place, even if you add something in your own words. Some teachers don't allow notes during a presentation, so check your class rules first; StageVoice is still a great way to rehearse.

How does voice tracking work?

StageVoice uses your phone's built-in speech recognition to turn what you say into words, then matches those words against your script to find your place. Because it follows your words rather than how loud you are, it moves when you read further and stays put when you pause.

What happens if I go off script or ad-lib?

It holds your place. The status changes to “Off script · holding” and the text stays still until you come back to your script; then it picks up at the line you return to, even if you skip a few words. You can also nudge the script up or down, or drag it, at any time.

Does StageVoice work offline?

Your scripts and Auto-Scroll work fully offline. Voice Tracking also works offline on phones that support on-device speech recognition. On other phones it uses the phone's built-in speech service, which may need an internet connection, and StageVoice tells you when that happens.

Is my voice recorded?

No. StageVoice does not record or save audio, and the app never sends your voice anywhere itself. Your phone's speech recognition turns speech into text, on the device when the phone supports it. Your scripts stay on your phone, and there is no account to create.

Does it work with teleprompter glass?

Yes. Mirror mode flips the text for beam-splitter teleprompter glass. You can also adjust text size, line spacing, high contrast and where the reading guide sits on the screen.

Can I still use a regular scrolling teleprompter?

Yes. Auto-Scroll is the classic constant-speed mode: set a speed from 40 to 300 words per minute, change it mid-speech, start with a 3-2-1 countdown and tap to pause. Switch between Auto-Scroll and Voice Tracking with one tap.

How much does StageVoice cost?

StageVoice Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, and both plans start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Prices are shown in the App Store and Google Play in your local currency. Your scripts are never locked: if your subscription ends, you can still open, edit and copy everything you wrote.
