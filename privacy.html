Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

StageVoice ("the app") is made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

StageVoice has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and does not sell personal data. Your scripts never leave your device. The only data that leaves your device is what is needed to run subscriptions: an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records, handled by our subscription provider, RevenueCat (see below).

Your scripts and settings

Scripts you type or paste, and your display settings, are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice Tracking (optional) uses your device's microphone so the script can follow what you say. It asks for microphone permission before first use.

  • Audio is passed to your device's built-in speech recognition service (Apple Speech on iOS, the system speech service on Android) only while Voice Tracking is listening. The app receives the recognized text, uses it to find your place in the script, and discards it.
  • The app does not record, save or transmit audio or transcripts.
  • When your device supports it, recognition happens on the device. On some devices the system speech service may process audio on its provider's servers (for example Apple or Google); that processing is governed by your device provider's privacy policy, not by this app.
  • You can turn off microphone access at any time in your device settings. Auto-Scroll and all other features keep working without it.

Subscriptions and purchases

Presenting scripts requires a StageVoice Pro subscription, bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details, name or email address.

To manage subscriptions we use RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider (processor). When the app starts and when you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous app user ID, randomly generated for this installation (not linked to your name, email or Apple/Google account details);
  • purchase information from the store: which plan, price and currency, purchase, renewal, trial and expiry dates, and store transaction IDs;
  • basic technical information needed to process requests: device platform and operating system version, app version, country/region and language settings, and your IP address (as part of any internet request).

We use this only to know whether your subscription is active, to restore it on a new installation, and to keep subscription records (for example for refunds and support). It is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat processes it under its privacy policy.

The app keeps a copy of your subscription status on your device so you can present while offline.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules one reminder on your device 2 days before the trial ends. It is created and shown by your phone; no data is sent to schedule it. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

Waitlist (this website)

If you join the waitlist, your email address is collected through our form provider, Formspree, and used only to tell you about the StageVoice launch. You can ask us to delete it at any time. This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Scripts and settings: delete them in the app, or delete the app.
  • Subscription records: Apple and Google keep their own purchase records. To ask us to delete the subscription records held by RevenueCat for your installation, email us (see Contact); include your approximate purchase date and store so we can find them. We may need to keep some records where the law requires (for example tax).
  • Waitlist: email us to be removed.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
